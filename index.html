Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Tarot AI is designed to be private by architecture, not by policy:

  • No data collection. All readings are generated on your device using Apple's on-device intelligence. Your questions, readings and journal entries never leave your iPhone.
  • No accounts. The app requires no sign-in and creates no user profile.
  • No tracking or analytics. The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising and no third-party trackers.
  • No network access required. Tarot AI works fully offline, including in airplane mode.
  • Children's privacy. Since no data is collected, no data from children is collected either.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at the support link below.

Support

Having trouble with Tarot AI? Please check:

  • Tarot AI requires Apple Intelligence to be enabled on your device (iPhone 15 Pro or newer, iOS 26+).
  • If readings show a generic message, make sure Apple Intelligence is on and your device language is supported.

For anything else, open an issue on our GitHub issue tracker — we read everything.
